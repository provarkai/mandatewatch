## 1. What MandateWatch is

MandateWatch is a civic-accountability platform tracking elected officials in Nigeria between
elections: a directory of representatives, citizen-generated approval ratings, a public demands
board, and discussion threads. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle your data.

## 2. What MandateWatch is not

- **We are not affiliated with INEC, the Nigerian government, or any political party.**
  Representative identity data (name, party, constituency, term) is sourced from public records;
  approval ratings, demands, and discussion content are generated by platform users, not by us.
- **We do not verify the truth of citizen-submitted content.** A demand, a discussion post, or a
  Stewardship claim from a verified representative is that person's statement, not a fact we have
  independently confirmed. We record positions; we do not adjudicate them.
- **We are not an election-forecasting service.** Any favorability or sentiment figures shown on
  the platform (including for election aspirants, when that feature is active) reflect citizen
  opinion at a point in time, never a prediction of an election outcome.

## 3. Your account

- You need a valid email address to sign in. We use passwordless "magic link" sign-in — we never
  ask for or store a password.
- You're responsible for keeping access to your email account secure, since that's how sign-in
  works.
- The name/initials, phone number, state, and LGA you provide should be accurate — some platform
  features (filing a demand, the "my area" view) depend on your state/LGA being correct.

## 5. Voting and demand integrity

- One account, one vote per representative per metric, one upvote per demand or comment. Attempts
  to circumvent this (creating multiple accounts, automated/bot activity) violate these terms and
  may result in account restriction.
- Votes and demand upvotes are final once cast and cannot be retracted through the product.

## 6. Claiming a representative profile

If you submit a request to be recognized as a verified representative or authorized staff member,
you confirm that the information you provide is accurate and that you are actually authorized to
represent that office. Knowingly submitting a false claim is a serious violation of these terms
and may have legal consequences beyond account restriction, given the impersonation risk involved.

## 7. Acceptable use

You agree not to:
- Impersonate any person, including an elected official you have not been verified to represent.
- Use the platform to distribute content that is unlawful under applicable Nigerian law, including
  defamatory content about a named individual.
- Attempt to circumvent vote-integrity or claim-verification mechanisms.
- Use automated means to scrape or bulk-extract platform data outside of any officially published
  export/API product (see MPAS-v2.md Chapter 17), once one exists.

## 8. No political payment for treatment

No political party, candidate, or officeholder can pay MandateWatch for different treatment,
ranking, or visibility of their data. This is a structural commitment, not a marketing claim — see
our platform principles.
